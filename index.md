# SELMA

The **SELMA** initiative (for _Saclaysian exchange on logic, models, and applications_) aims to bring together research on the Saclay plateau on topics related to logic, semantics and formal methods, ranging from theoretical approaches (type theory, category theory, algebraic topology, set-theoretic methods and abstract interpretation) to practice (proof assistants, automated verification tools, for programs, distributed systems, CPS, AI). The core of the group leading this initiative consists of

- the [_Logic, models and formal methods_](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/proofs-algorithms/) division of LIX (École polytechnique), which includes the Cosynus, Partout and PhIQuS teams,
- the [_Models_](https://lmf.cnrs.fr/Research/Models) division of LMF (ENS Paris-Saclay), which includes the Distributed Computing and Model Checking and Synthesis teams.

The goal of this group is to meet twice a month in order to run

- a working group (series of sessions presenting papers around a given topic),
- a seminar with national and international guests.

These joint working sessions should strengthen the natural but still fragmented scientific ties between these teams and eventually foster new scientific collaborations. The seminars will be held alternately at ENS Paris-Saclay and at École polytechnique, and will build on the existing team seminars.
